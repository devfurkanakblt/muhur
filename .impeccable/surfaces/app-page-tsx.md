Scope: whole app (landing/countdown, import flow, project form, team invite, gallery, admin). Visitor mode: Operate (gallery leans Experience).

Audience: DEÜ CS&AI hackathon teams on laptops at the deadline; admins/jury; public visitors.
Job: import a public repo, fill four fields, see certainty of registration; admins lock and download archives.
Constraints: strict minimalism (user-pinned), mascot palette (near-black, indigo, pink blush, cool off-white), Turkish copy.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The hackathon is an exhibition being installed. Until the deadline the vitrine is open and teams arrange their piece; at the lock a glass pane closes and every project becomes a catalogued exhibit with a museum label. Refuses the default card-grid SaaS dashboard with badges and gradients.

OWN-WORLD: Cool gallery-wall off-white ground, charcoal ink, one indigo that means only "deadline / lock / glass" (raise from orienteering: course purple reserved for the active leg). Pink only as mascot blush and a single "registered" seal. Tombstone-label typography: name in a firm sans, small tracked caps for field names, tabular mono for SHA, timestamps and countdown digits right-aligned tight (raise from j-card and exposure record). Every logo sits at one fixed scale on one identical plinth square, sharing one baseline (raise from botanical folio). Hairline rules, no shadows, no cards-in-cards.

STORY: Visitor sees the countdown and the mascot, understands "submit before the glass closes", connects GitHub, picks a public repo, fills four fields, gets a label with their exhibit number. After lock they see the sealed label with frozen SHA.
